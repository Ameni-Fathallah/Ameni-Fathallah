### About me

I'm a Master's student in **Artificial Intelligence, Data & Agentic** at Université Paris Dauphine-PSL. Before moving into AI, I spent two internships shipping full-stack web products, so I like taking models all the way to something people can actually use.

These days I mostly work on **retrieval-augmented generation, LLM agents and NLP**, with a solid base in classical machine learning.

- 🔭 Currently: M2 AIDA at Dauphine, contributing to the FAME research benchmark on LLM investment agents
- 🎯 Looking for: a **6-month end-of-studies internship from February 2027** in Data Science, AI Engineering or ML
- 🏆 Graduated first in my class (Bachelor in Computer Science, highest honors)
- 🤾 Off-screen: 11 years of handball and a lot of pastry experiments
